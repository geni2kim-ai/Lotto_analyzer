# Lotto Insight Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: August 10, 2026**

Genie Kim ("Developer") values user privacy and complies with applicable privacy laws and regulations. This Privacy Policy describes how user data is handled in the Lotto Insight application ("App").

## 1. Data Collection
- The App operates locally without requiring account creation or login.
- No personally identifiable information (name, email, phone number, location, device identifier) is collected, transmitted, or stored on external servers.
- **Local Storage**: User-configured weighting parameters and generated recommendation histories are stored exclusively within the device's local Room database.

## 2. Third-Party Sharing
- The App does not sell, trade, or share user data with third parties.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
- All application data resides strictly on the local device.
- Uninstalling the App immediately removes all stored data, configuration settings, and recommendation histories.
